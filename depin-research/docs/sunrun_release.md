## Key facts (from search-result coverage — verify against the PDF)

- Announced 2026-07-08; Sunrun's first move into distributed edge computing,
  described as a high-margin new business category.
- Expands a proof-of-concept: compute nodes placed in homes with Sunrun solar
  + battery systems; nodes are paired with onsite batteries and can ride
  through certain grid outages.
- Sunrun coordinates **selling inference capacity to enterprise compute
  buyers** and tests nodes under varied conditions and rate structures.
- Homeowners are compensated for hosting nodes.
- Positioning: uses existing grid infrastructure rather than adding
  concentrated data-center load; claims faster capacity addition than
  traditional data centers.
- Pilot to run "over the coming months" against defined milestones before a
  broader rollout decision.
