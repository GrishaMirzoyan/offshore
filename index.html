Where we're rigid

Every structure we build is fully reportable and legal in your home jurisdiction. We verify every client. If you're looking to hide money, we're not your firm.

Questions

Asked frequently. Answered dryly.

Is this legal?

Yes. Boringly so. Every structure we build is fully reportable in your home jurisdiction — and we prepare the reports. The exciting kind of offshore is the kind that ends in a documentary. We practice the other kind.

Do I still pay taxes at home?

Almost certainly, yes. Anyone who promises otherwise is selling you a future legal problem. What a proper structure changes is where your assets sit, which legal systems protect them, and how many filing deadlines you personally carry. Our target for that last number is zero.

Am I a fit?

If your life's work sits inside a single country's system and you'd prefer it didn't, probably. The structuring call exists to answer this honestly — and sometimes the honest answer is "not yet." We'd rather be the firm you come back to.
